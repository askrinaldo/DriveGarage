Act as a senior SaaS billing architect, backend engineer, and product security lead.

Goal:
Prepare DriveGarage for Vipps subscription billing and access control.

Important:

* Do not implement real Vipps API unless credentials/docs are available.

Business model:

* 7-day free trial
* then 100 NOK/month

Internal fair-use limits:

* max 10 vehicles
* max 200 receipts/documents
* max 50 AI requests/month
* max 25 PDF exports/month
* max 10 club memberships
* max 2 owned clubs

Do not show GB/MB.

Subscription states:

* trialing
* pending_vipps_agreement
* active
* past_due
* payment_failed
* canceled
* expired
* exempt_internal
* deletion_requested
* deleted

Access rules:

* trialing: full access
* active: full access
* exempt_internal: full access
* pending_vipps_agreement: app access with billing reminder
* past_due/payment_failed: limited access with payment recovery message
* canceled: access until paid/trial period ends
* expired: locked app, but billing/account/export/legal still accessible
* deletion_requested: locked except account/support
* deleted: no login/data access

Data retention rules:

* Failed payment or canceled subscription must NOT delete data immediately.
* Keep data for at least 90 days after expiration.
* User can reactivate and recover access during retention period.
* Account deletion is different from subscription cancellation.
* Account deletion should require explicit confirmation.
* Account deletion should delete/anonymize personal garage data after a grace period, for example 7–30 days.
* Keep only legally required minimal audit/billing records.
